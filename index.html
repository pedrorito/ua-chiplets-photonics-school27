About the School

An intensive school dedicated to the packaging of electronic and photonic devices, with a forward-looking perspective on the field. The program covers an overview of packaging, electro-optical characterization techniques (case studies), photonic devices (case studies), and software tools for chiplet packaging. It combines lectures with hands-on small-group sessions and complementary activities. The school is national in scope and targets students with a bachelor's degree in STEM field.

Organised by the University of Aveiro (UA) and funded by POEMS – Portuguese Competence Centre in Semiconductors. POEMS is co-funded by Chips JU (grant 101217646) and FCT.

Is it for you?

Who should attend and why

Who should attend?

  • Students, early-career researchers and industry professionals seeking to expand their expertise in packaging of electronic and photonic devices.
  • Technician experts wanting to strengthen their competencies.
  • Anyone passionate about packaging of electronic and photonic devices.

Why attend?

  • Learn from leading experts on packaging of electronic and photonic devices.
  • Explore emerging open software tools for chiplet packaging.
  • Gain hands-on experience with electro-optical characterisation techniques.
What you'll learn

Topics

Packaging fundamentals

Core concepts of device packaging for electronic and photonic systems.

Chiplets & integration

How different components come together in one package.

Characterisation

Electro-optical characterisation techniques, through case studies and in the lab.

Photonic devices

An overview of photonic devices and their place in packaged systems.

Design & simulation tools

A first look at software tools used in chiplet packaging.

Hands-on practice

Small-group practical sessions with close interaction with trainers.

Applications

Who can apply

This school is designed for Portuguese STEM students and professionals with an undergraduate degree who are considering expanding their expertise in packaging of electronic and photonic devices. Students from all STEM disciplines (Science, Technology, Engineering and Mathematics) enrolled at any Portuguese university are welcome to apply.

Proficiency in English is essential, as all sessions are conducted in English.

Eligibility

  • MSc and PhD students in any STEM field (Science, Technology, Engineering, Mathematics), studying or working in Portugal, of any nationality
  • Company staff with a minimum education level compatible with an MSc in STEM

Selection

Selection is based on the applicant's academic and/or professional record and motivation letter. We are committed to ensuring diversity in terms of gender, geographical representation, and academic/professional backgrounds.

  • Academic record / CV
  • Motivation letter
  • Recommendation letter (optional)
  • Gender and geographic diversity is taken into account – limited to 25 places
